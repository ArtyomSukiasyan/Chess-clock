Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

This policy describes how Chess Clock — the chess clock available on the web and as an Android app — handles your information. The short version: the app does not collect, store on a server, or share any personal data.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. There are no accounts, sign-ups, analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind, and the app does not use cookies.

Information stored on your device

The app saves the time control you last chose (minutes, seconds and increment for each player) in your browser's or the app's local storage, so it is ready next time you play. This never leaves your device and is not sent to us or anyone else. You can delete it at any time by clearing the site data in your browser, or by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

The state of a game in progress, such as the remaining time on each clock, is kept only in memory and is lost when you close the app.

Hosting

The web version is hosted on Vercel. Like any web host, Vercel may automatically process technical information such as your IP address and browser type to deliver the page and protect against abuse. We do not access or use this information. The Android app includes the clock itself and works offline, so playing in the app does not send requests to Vercel.

App permissions

The Android app requests only internet access, which is a standard permission for apps built on a web view. It does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, files, or any other personal data. While the app is open, it keeps the screen on so it doesn't turn off in the middle of a game; this does not require any permission.

Children's privacy

Chess Clock is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no personal information, it does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
